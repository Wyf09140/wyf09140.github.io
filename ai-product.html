How I work

Start from user data

Before changing the support chatbot, I clustered 100K+ real sessions to see where it failed. Fixes go where the data points.

Ship small, then iterate

The vulnerability pipeline began as a simple n8n alert and grew into LLM triage. The admissions chatbot went through two versions with the client.

Verify every AI output

LLMs are fast and sometimes wrong. Nothing they produce reaches production until it has been checked.

Bridge engineering and the business

I’ve been the go-between for an AI vendor and IT, led a system from vendor evaluation to production alongside a PM, and presented products to non-technical leaders.
