SMB advertiser health analysis

Real data from Google Ads Transparency Center, using the official public dump (BigQuery: bigquery-public-data.google_ads_transparency_center.creative_stats). Built in Python.

Business question

Long-tail advertisers are the largest group, but they contribute little reach and churn fast. Where should a scaled sales team focus its activation and retention effort?

Data and method

  • 2,644 ads from 1,734 advertisers shown in Germany, France, the UK, Italy, and the EEA, first shown from March 2023 to November 2026.
  • Advertisers tiered by estimated reach: Top 10%, Mid 40%, and Long tail 50%.
  • Churn is defined as no ad shown in the last 60 days of the data window.
  • Monthly cohort retention curves (M0 to M6), and weekly new-ad anomaly flags using an 8-week rolling baseline and z-score.

Key findings

  • Reach is highly concentrated: the top 10% hold 91%, and the long-tail 50% hold 0.4%.
  • Churn falls as advertisers grow: 66% in the long tail, 57% in the mid tier, and 52% in the top tier.
  • Long-tail advertisers run about 1 ad each, compared with 3.4 for the top tier.
  • The verticals with the highest churn (5+ advertisers) are mobile games at about 77%, furniture at about 70%, and fashion at about 64%. Skincare and fitness are lower, at about 43–44%.
  • Typical cohorts fall to 30–40% active by month 3.

Recommendations

  • Run early-life activation outreach in an advertiser’s first 90 days, before long-tail churn sets in.
  • Build re-engagement plays for each high-churn vertical, starting with mobile games and furniture.
  • Use the weekly new-ad anomaly monitor as an early-warning signal for the sales team.

Limitations

This is Google data, not TikTok data. Reach is a range estimate, and there is no spend or conversion data. Verticals are mapped from advertiser-name keywords. The sample comes from one data shard and is not random. The framework itself transfers directly to TikTok’s Ad Library.

Cross-platform ads performance practice

Practice project on a synthetic Kaggle dataset. The results are not business findings.

What I built

A pandas and matplotlib pipeline over 1,800 daily rows (Google, Meta, and TikTok, 2024): comparisons by platform, industry, and country on CTR and CPC, an 8-week rolling z-score monitor on weekly spend, and 9 charts. The pipeline is built to run on real platform data.
